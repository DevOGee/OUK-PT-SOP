CHAPTER I

On-Boarding Milestones

Before instruction can commence, the University requires the completion of several vital administrative and technical milestones.

Administrative Setup

  • Receipt and signing of the PT Contract Letter.
  • Activation of the official OUK Faculty Email via ICT portal.
  • Submission of a professional passport photograph.

Technical Integration

  • Acquisition of MyOUK login credentials.
  • Verification of SOMAS LMS access.
  • Acceptance of the Zoom Teaching Licence.
  • Configuration of the Panopto web client.
FACULTY REQUIREMENT Full system access must be validated at least seven days prior to the commencement of teaching.

CHAPTER II

Instruction & Facilitation

Instruction at OUK is a digital-first experience. Faculty are expected to maintain the highest standards of course shell excellence.

Pre-Teaching Validation

Faculty must meticulously audit their assigned course shell for accuracy in content, hyperlink functionality, and activity settings.

Functional Requirements

  • Configuration of Zoom Live Class links within SOMAS.
  • Enabling the Panopto block for automatic session recording.
  • Setting strict Restriction Settings for assessments.
  • Defining Completion Tracking parameters.
STRICT POLICY Deletion of institutional content is prohibited. All materials must remain within the SOMAS ecosystem.

CHAPTER III

Assessment Guidelines

The digital workflow for grading must be followed without exception to ensure institutional integrity.

Gradebook Configuration

  • Weightings: CATTs (50%) and Final Examination (50%).
  • All grading must occur within the LMS using rubrics.

Examinations

  • Preparation of dual examination versions (Mid & End of Session).
  • Secure LMS digitization and invigilation configuration.
  • Session moderation must be conducted strictly within the LMS.

CHAPTER IV

Marks Capture Process

Official submission of marks is the final mandatory stage of the instructional cycle.

  1. Export current grades from the SOMAS Gradebook.
  2. Access the MyOUK Examination Portal.
  3. Input marks manually or via the Excel Bulk Upload template.
  4. Generate the official Submission Report as proof.
SUBMISSION PROOF The Submission Report must be electronically shared with your Head of Department immediately upon completion.

CHAPTER V

Maintenance & Evaluation

Continuous pedagogical improvement is vital for our university's growth.

  • Review student performance data and feedback.
  • Propose content updates for subsequent intakes.
  • Participate in post-session faculty reflection meetings.
